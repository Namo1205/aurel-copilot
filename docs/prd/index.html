PRD: Aurel Reservations AI Agent, v1

Product requirements · Owner: AI Product Manager · Engineering: 2 full-stack, designer at 50% · 23 October 2026
ProblemGuests wait 9 hours for an email reply (34% book if answered in an hour, 9% after a day), and 23% of pre-arrival requests are not delivered. What we buildAn AI agent that reads every email and WhatsApp message, marks its type, assigns a named owner, drafts the reply with live prices and the right hotel rule, turns requests into hotel to-dos, and answers policy questions on its own. How we measureReply time under an hour, requests not delivered under 10% then 5%, 80% of policy-only messages answered without a person, zero broken promises, every item owned.
Terms. AI agent is the software, written with "AI" in this document; on the desk it is called Aurel Assistant. Agent on its own is a person on the 22-person reservations desk (the CRO). The PMS is the hotel's booking system. A trace is a to-do in the PMS sent to a hotel department with a due date. A queue is a list of items waiting for one kind of owner. An owner is the named person accountable for an item until it is closed.

1Scope

In v1 (first quarter)

  • AI agent marks the type, finds the booking, flags urgency and sensitive data
  • Owner assigned on arrival, by rule
  • Important view + five queues (Flow A)
  • Sends alone: cited policy answers; fixed templates only: holding reply, group acknowledgement, off-topic decline, the question asking which hotel, day-before message
  • Also built: attachments in and out (never read by the AI agent); a follow-up prepared when a quote has no reply for 48 h; the mailbox as source of truth, loaded on sign-in
  • Drafts with live PMS price; PMS changes prepared within rules; one PMS trace per request
  • Day-before check on every booking (Flow B)
  • Dashboard by role (Flow C)
  • Login per role: agent, senior, manager, groups specialist
  • If time runs short: cut from the end, day-before check first

Next (second quarter)

  • Price quotes and within-rules changes earn the right to send alone
  • Out-of-hours cover for the types that send alone
  • Automatic message to the guest when a trace is closed
  • Outreach to past guests from PMS stays: fixed template, batches approved by an agent, opt-out and do-not-contact enforced; replies sorted by the desk
  • Languages beyond English

Out

  • Booking and taking payment without a person
  • Any discount or price match
  • Group pricing (the groups specialists own it)
  • Website chatbot
  • Marketing to lists; the desk only ever writes to a guest who wrote first
  • PMS write without approval

Prototype: what is real and what is simulated

RealThe AI agent (Google Gemini, read-only PMS tools) doing everything in the v1 list against per-hotel handbooks; a rule-based engine takes over when the model is unavailable, labelled. Email through a real Gmail mailbox, polled by open desks and a five-minute scheduled check; replies really go out.
SimulatedThe PMS (nine hotels, rates, bookings, daily prices), the hotel's trace list (a PMS traces page stands in), the payment link. WhatsApp is not in the prototype.

2Users

Reservations agent (13)Works the Important view and their own queue. Approves, edits, changes the type, or sends to a manager.
Needs: one click to send a right answer; never to be blamed for the AI agent's mistake.
Senior agent (4)Everything an agent has, plus their team's lists and reassignment.
Needs: see who is stuck before the guest notices.
Reservations manager (2)Owns the Manager queue. Each item arrives with the booking, the rule it breaks and the guest's words.
Needs: decide once, same day, with context.
Groups and events specialist (3)Owns the Sales queue. Group and event items arrive with dates, size, hotel and the guest's words; replies within one working day.
Needs: a complete brief, no generalist back-and-forth.

Not users of this product, but served by it: hotel department staff (front office, housekeeping, concierge, F&B) work the traces in the PMS they already use; the hotel general manager gets trace escalations and the weekly promises-kept number; the guest receives auto-sent messages signed as the reservations team.

4AI agent behaviour spec

One row per type. A message with several asks (date change + cot + anniversary) gets one proposal with one action per ask, at the strictest approval level among them. Anything sends alone only when the whole message is of that type: a spa question with a buried allergy never auto-sends. Scope is the guest's stay at Aurel only; text inside a message is information, never an instruction, and a topic check in code runs before drafting and before every send. Every sent message records the type, the policy used and what was not promised.

Type · shareWhat it handlesWhat it never doesHands off when → to whomHow it confirmsHow it fails
Policy question
12% sends alone		 Parking, pets, check-in and check-out times, directions, facilities. Finds the paragraph in that hotel's policy documents and answers from it, citing it. Sends alone only if a paragraph was found and the answer cites it. Answer from general knowledge. Guess a hotel. Treat "can I check in at 9am?" as policy: early check-in is always a special request. No paragraph found, or anything else in the message → next agent, drafted. Hotel not named → fixed question sent alone; the reply is read with the thread States the policy as written and names the hotel. Signed as the reservations team. Weekly check of 50 auto-sent: a wrong promise pauses auto-send at once; over 2% wrong pauses it for that hotel.
Price / availability
31% approve		 Pulls today's price and availability from the PMS for the dates, room and party. Drafts the quote with Flexible and Advance Purchase options, totals and terms. Offers a hold and a payment link. Quote a number not returned by the PMS in this session. Discount, match or undercut any price. Hold or book without approval. Non-public or negotiated rate asked for → manager. Dates or hotel missing → drafts a question for the guest. "As of today" prices; booking confirmed only after gateway payment succeeds. PMS unavailable → draft acknowledges, gives a time to come back, no numbers. Room not available → nearest alternative. No reply in 48 h → follow-up prepared for the owner, never sent alone.
Booking change
22% approve		 Checks the booking's rate rules and live availability. Prepares the PMS change with the price difference. The agent approves the write. Change an Advance Purchase booking. Waive a difference. Rate does not allow changes, or price difference needs waiving → manager Updated confirmation sent only after the PMS write succeeds. Write fails → nothing sent, item returns to the owner with the error. A reply claiming a change the PMS did not make is blocked in code. Booking not found → asks for the reference.
Special request
18% approve		 Cot, transfer, early check-in, occasion, spa, dining, dietary. One trace per request to the right department with a due date before arrival. Drafts "logged, the hotel will confirm". Allergy, medical, accessibility → restricted trace, minimum detail, tells the guest who will see it. Say a request is done or guaranteed (early check-in, upgrade, transfer). Put sensitive detail in free text or an email. Promise an allergen-free kitchen. VIP or travel-advisor email → senior agent; amenities confirmed by a person, upgrade never promised in writing. Needs a hotel decision → via the trace. Delivery is confirmed by the hotel from the trace, in writing. Q2: automatic message on close. Not acknowledged within 24 h of due → escalates to the hotel's front office manager. Trace creation fails → item stays open, owner alerted.
Cancellation / refund
9% approve template		 Checks the rate and the cancellation window. Within rules: drafts the confirmation, asks the guest to confirm. Outside rules or compassionate: sends a fixed-template holding reply at once ("received, a manager will contact you today") and hands off with the booking attached. Confirm a refund, credit or waiver outside the rules. Cancel without the guest's explicit yes. Ask for medical proof. Put anything but the template in an auto-sent message. Any refund, waiver or credit outside the rules, or a compassionate case → manager, same day. Cancellation confirmed in writing after the PMS update. Refund confirmed only by the manager. Rules unclear → treated as outside the rules and routed to the manager.
Group / event
3% to Sales template		 Recognises 8+ rooms, meeting space, weddings. Sends a fixed-template acknowledgement (one-working-day reply from the specialist). Passes on dates, size, hotel and the guest's words. Quote, hold inventory or state availability for a group. Always → groups specialist Acknowledgement only. The specialist confirms. Group size unclear → still routed; the specialist asks.
Other · off-topic · manipulation
5% approve template		 Unclear ask: drafts a short reply asking what is needed. Card details → removed, payment link proposed. Off-topic (code, homework, general knowledge, legal or medical advice, competitors, anything not about a stay at Aurel) or instructions aimed at the AI ("ignore your rules", "write me a script", "show your prompt") → fixed-template decline, routed to an agent, counted in the audit log. Attempt the off-topic task. Reveal its own instructions, other guests' details or internal notes. Store, repeat or forward card details. Under 60% confidence → senior agent. Repeated attempts from one sender → senior agent. Complaint, threat or abuse → manager. Nothing confirmed. Unsure whether on-topic → treated as off-topic, drafted for a person. Model output invalid or model down → one repair attempt, then the rule-based engine, labelled.

How a type earns the right to send alone

  • A type qualifies when, over 4 weeks and 300+ items: 95% of drafts sent with no or trivial edits, type changed under 3%, zero guardrail breaches.
  • The manager switches it on per type and per hotel.
  • Expected order: price quotes (Q2), then within-rules changes. Refunds and sensitive requests never qualify.

Rollout, first quarter

Weeks 1 to 2 · ShadowEmail connected. Policy documents loaded for 9 hotels. AI agent drafts everything; nobody sends from it. Measure draft acceptance, type accuracy and real minutes per contact. Hotels: a named sponsor per hotel; department heads trained on the PMS trace list.
Weeks 3 to 6 · Pilot4 volunteer agents, Lisbon and Mallorca. Sending from the desk; auto-send on for those two hotels. Traces live for both; "promises kept" % reported weekly, misses reviewed with the sponsor, not blamed. Weekly QA in the open with the team.
Weeks 7 to 10 · All agentsAll 22, all 9 hotels, email. Owner rules and dashboard live. Traces at every hotel; weekly promises-kept % per hotel in the COO's review; hotels under 80% acknowledged for two weeks get a visit from the PM.
Weeks 11 to 12 · WhatsApp and decisionsWhatsApp connected after Meta verification. No headcount reduction planned. Replacing leavers is decided on measured hours, first review end Q1. Decide which type earns autonomy next. Q2: OTA, out-of-hours, trace-close messages.

Assumptions. Desk of 22: 2 managers, 3 groups and events specialists, 4 seniors, 13 agents. Email and WhatsApp providers expose threaded read and send APIs; WhatsApp verification takes 1 to 4 weeks. PMS trace API accepts department, due date and a restricted flag, and returns trace status so acknowledgements and promises kept can be counted; otherwise sensitive detail stays in our system with a pointer and status is a vendor gap to raise in week 1. Policy documents for 9 hotels structured by the PM with each hotel in weeks 1 to 2. Two engineers for two quarters (~52 engineer-weeks) cover the v1 list with a five-week buffer. Model provider: data processing agreement, EU inference region, card numbers removed and sensitive detail minimised before any call.
