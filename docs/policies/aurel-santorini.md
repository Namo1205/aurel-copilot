# Aurel Santorini — Hotel handbook

Hotel: SAN

Caldera-edge resort in Oia, Santorini. Rooms: Cave Room, Sea View Room, Plunge Pool Suite. Group rules in the group handbook apply, with the exception on children below.

## Check-in and check-out times
<!-- id: P-SAN-HOURS · tags: check-in, check-out, time, times, hours, arrival, departure, luggage -->
Check-in at Aurel Santorini is from 15:00 and check-out is by 11:00. Luggage can be stored before check-in and after check-out, and guests may use the pool, spa and restaurant from arrival. Porters carry luggage down the caldera steps. Reception is open 07:00 to 23:00, with a night porter after that.

## Facilities
<!-- id: P-SAN-FACILITIES · tags: gym, fitness, pool, spa, wifi, wi-fi, breakfast, facilities, restaurant, bar, sunset -->
Aurel Santorini has an outdoor infinity pool, a fitness room open 06:00 to 22:00, a spa with treatment rooms and a thermal area, a restaurant and a sunset bar, and complimentary Wi-Fi throughout. Breakfast is served 07:30 to 11:00 and can be served on the suite terrace. The hotel closes from November to March.

## Parking
<!-- id: P-SAN-PARK · tags: parking, car, garage, drive, quad, scooter -->
Free parking in the hotel car park at the top of Oia, a 5-minute walk and about 90 steps from the hotel; porters collect luggage from the car park. Rental cars, quads and scooters can be delivered there by the concierge's partner.

## Pets
<!-- id: P-SAN-PETS · tags: pets, pet, dog, dogs, cat, animal -->
Pets are not accepted at Aurel Santorini, except certified assistance animals, which stay free. The caldera steps and open terraces make the site unsuitable for animals.

## Airport transfers
<!-- id: P-SAN-TRANSFER · tags: transfer, airport, pick up, driver, car, taxi, port, ferry -->
A private transfer from Santorini airport or the Athinios port is EUR 70 per car for up to three guests, 30 to 40 minutes. Confirmed by the concierge once the flight or ferry number is received. Santorini has very few taxis in summer, so we recommend pre-booking.

## Spa bookings
<!-- id: P-SAN-SPA · tags: spa, treatment, massage, wellness, thermal, couples -->
Spa treatments and in-suite couples' massages can be pre-booked by the concierge up to the day before arrival. Guests under 16 are not admitted to the thermal area. Treatments cancelled with less than 24 hours' notice are charged in full.

## Nearby dining
<!-- id: P-SAN-DINING · tags: restaurant, dining, dinner, recommend, eat, sunset -->
The concierge recommends Ammoudi Fish Tavern (by the water, 300 steps down), Lauda (fine dining, 3 minutes' walk) and Metaxi Mas near Exo Gonia (traditional, 20 minutes by car). Sunset tables in Oia must be booked a week ahead in summer.

## Sea view guarantee
<!-- id: P-SAN-VIEW · tags: sea view, view, caldera, room allocation, terrace -->
Sea View Rooms and Plunge Pool Suites face the caldera and have a private terrace. Cave Rooms face the courtyard. The view category is guaranteed; the specific room is allocated on the day of arrival.

## Children
<!-- id: P-SAN-CHILD · tags: children, child, kids, age, cot, baby, family, minimum age -->
Due to the cliffside terraces and unfenced plunge pools, Aurel Santorini welcomes children aged 12 and over only. Cots and extra beds are not available. Families with younger children are offered Aurel Algarve or Aurel Comporta instead.

## Accessibility
<!-- id: P-SAN-ACCESS · tags: accessibility, wheelchair, step-free, mobility, steps -->
Aurel Santorini is reached by about 90 steps from the car park and all rooms involve further steps; there are no lifts and no step-free rooms. The hotel is not suitable for wheelchair users or guests with limited mobility, and the desk says so plainly rather than recording a request the hotel cannot meet.
