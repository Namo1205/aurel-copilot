# Aurel Roma — Hotel handbook

Hotel: ROM

City hotel near Piazza Navona, Rome. Rooms: Classic Room, Deluxe Room, Terrace Suite. Group rules in the group handbook apply.

## Check-in and check-out times
<!-- id: P-ROM-HOURS · tags: check-in, check-out, time, times, hours, arrival, departure, luggage -->
Check-in at Aurel Roma is from 15:00 and check-out is by 11:00. Luggage can be stored before check-in and after check-out, and guests may use the fitness room and bar from arrival. Reception is open 24 hours.

## Facilities
<!-- id: P-ROM-FACILITIES · tags: gym, fitness, pool, spa, wifi, wi-fi, breakfast, facilities, restaurant, bar, rooftop -->
Aurel Roma has a fitness room open 24 hours, complimentary Wi-Fi throughout, a restaurant and a rooftop bar open 18:00 to midnight from April to October. Breakfast is served 07:00 to 10:30. There is no swimming pool or spa.

## Parking
<!-- id: P-ROM-PARK · tags: parking, car, garage, valet, drive, ztl -->
There is no on-site parking. A partner garage 300 m away charges EUR 45 per day; the concierge can reserve a space with the car's registration number. The hotel sits inside Rome's restricted traffic zone (ZTL); the concierge must register the registration number before arrival or the car will be fined.

## Pets
<!-- id: P-ROM-PETS · tags: pets, pet, dog, dogs, cat, animal -->
Pets are not accepted at Aurel Roma, except certified assistance animals, which stay free in any room. The concierge can recommend a nearby pet hotel.

## Airport transfers
<!-- id: P-ROM-TRANSFER · tags: transfer, airport, pick up, driver, car, taxi, fiumicino, ciampino -->
A private transfer from Fiumicino is EUR 80 per car for up to three guests, 45 to 60 minutes; from Ciampino EUR 70. The concierge confirms it once the flight number is received. Official taxis charge a fixed EUR 55 from Fiumicino to the centre.

## Nearby dining
<!-- id: P-ROM-DINING · tags: restaurant, dining, dinner, recommend, eat, trattoria -->
The concierge recommends Armando al Pantheon (Roman classics, 5 minutes' walk, book two weeks ahead), Pierluigi (seafood, 6 minutes' walk) and Roscioli for lunch. Tables are booked by the concierge.

## Accessibility
<!-- id: P-ROM-ACCESS · tags: accessibility, wheelchair, step-free, accessible room, lift, mobility -->
The building is sixteenth century; one Deluxe Room on the first floor is fully step-free with a roll-in shower and is reached by the lift. The rooftop bar has three steps. Access needs are recorded in a restricted trace to front office so the right room is blocked before arrival.

## Getting around
<!-- id: P-ROM-LOCAL · tags: location, neighbourhood, directions, sights, walk, noise -->
Piazza Navona and the Pantheon are 3 minutes on foot, the Vatican 20 minutes. Rooms facing the inner courtyard are the quietest; Terrace Suites face the street and have a private terrace.
