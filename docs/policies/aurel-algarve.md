# Aurel Algarve — Hotel handbook

Hotel: ALG

Family resort in Lagos, Algarve. Rooms: Garden Room, Sea View Room, Family Suite. Group rules in the group handbook apply.

## Check-in and check-out times
<!-- id: P-ALG-HOURS · tags: check-in, check-out, time, times, hours, arrival, departure, luggage -->
Check-in at Aurel Algarve is from 15:00 and check-out is by 12:00. Luggage can be stored before check-in and after check-out, and guests may use the pools, spa and beach from arrival; changing rooms are in the spa. Reception is open 24 hours.

## Facilities
<!-- id: P-ALG-FACILITIES · tags: gym, fitness, pool, spa, wifi, wi-fi, breakfast, facilities, restaurant, bar, beach, tennis -->
Aurel Algarve has two outdoor pools (one heated and one for children), a fitness room open 06:00 to 22:00, a spa with treatment rooms and a thermal area, two tennis courts, direct beach access and complimentary Wi-Fi throughout. Breakfast is served 07:30 to 11:00. The restaurant and pool bar serve all day.

## Parking
<!-- id: P-ALG-PARK · tags: parking, car, garage, drive, rental car, electric charging -->
Free parking on site for all guests, with electric charging at no extra cost. A car is useful for the western beaches; the concierge can arrange a rental delivered to the hotel.

## Pets
<!-- id: P-ALG-PETS · tags: pets, pet, dog, dogs, cat, animal -->
Dogs up to 20 kg are welcome in Garden Rooms at EUR 40 per night, maximum one per room, and may use the dog-friendly beach 10 minutes' walk away. Dogs are not accepted in Sea View Rooms or Family Suites and are not admitted to the pool areas or restaurant. Other animals are not accepted except certified assistance animals, which stay free.

## Airport transfers
<!-- id: P-ALG-TRANSFER · tags: transfer, airport, pick up, driver, car, taxi, faro -->
A private transfer from Faro airport is EUR 110 per car for up to three guests, about 60 minutes; a minivan for up to six is EUR 160. Confirmed by the concierge once the flight number is received.

## Spa bookings
<!-- id: P-ALG-SPA · tags: spa, treatment, massage, wellness, thermal -->
Spa treatments can be pre-booked by the concierge up to the day before arrival. Guests under 16 are not admitted to the thermal area. Treatments cancelled with less than 24 hours' notice are charged in full.

## Nearby dining
<!-- id: P-ALG-DINING · tags: restaurant, dining, dinner, recommend, eat, seafood -->
The concierge recommends Dom Henrique (seafood in the old town, 10 minutes by taxi), Avenida (modern Portuguese on the marina) and O Camilo above Praia do Camilo for lunch. Tables are booked by the concierge.

## Children and families
<!-- id: P-ALG-CHILD · tags: children, child, kids, family, cot, babysitting, kids club -->
Children of all ages are welcome. Family Suites sleep two adults and up to three children; cots fit in every room type. A kids' club runs 09:30 to 17:30 daily from June to September for ages 4 to 12, free of charge, and the children's pool is lifeguarded in July and August. Babysitting with 24 hours' notice.

## Accessibility
<!-- id: P-ALG-ACCESS · tags: accessibility, wheelchair, step-free, accessible room, mobility, beach -->
Four Garden Rooms and two Family Suites are step-free with roll-in showers; all public areas are level or ramped. The beach is reached by a ramp and a beach wheelchair is available on request. Access needs are recorded in a restricted trace to front office so the right room is blocked before arrival.
