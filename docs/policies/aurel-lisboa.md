# Aurel Lisboa — Hotel handbook

Hotel: LIS

City hotel in Chiado, Lisbon. Rooms: Deluxe Room, River View Room, Tagus Suite. Group rules in the group handbook apply.

## Check-in and check-out times
<!-- id: P-LIS-HOURS · tags: check-in, check-out, time, times, hours, arrival, departure, luggage -->
Check-in at Aurel Lisboa is from 15:00 and check-out is by 12:00. Luggage can be stored before check-in and after check-out, and guests may use the fitness room and bar from arrival. Reception is open 24 hours.

## Facilities
<!-- id: P-LIS-FACILITIES · tags: gym, fitness, pool, spa, wifi, wi-fi, breakfast, facilities, restaurant, bar, rooftop -->
Aurel Lisboa has a fitness room open 24 hours, complimentary Wi-Fi throughout, a restaurant and a rooftop bar with views over the Tagus, open 17:00 to 01:00. Breakfast is served 07:00 to 10:30 in the restaurant, until 11:00 at weekends. There is no swimming pool or spa; in-room massages can be arranged by the concierge.

## Parking
<!-- id: P-LIS-PARK · tags: parking, car, valet, garage, drive, electric charging -->
Valet parking is available at EUR 35 per night; hand the keys to the doorman on arrival, no reservation needed. Electric charging is available on request at no extra cost. The streets of Chiado are narrow and partly pedestrian, so we recommend arriving by taxi.

## Pets
<!-- id: P-LIS-PETS · tags: pets, pet, dog, dogs, cat, animal -->
Dogs up to 12 kg are welcome in Deluxe and River View rooms at EUR 40 per night, maximum one per room. A bed and bowls are provided. Dogs are not admitted to the restaurant but are welcome on the rooftop terrace. Cats and other animals are not accepted except certified assistance animals, which stay free in any room.

## Airport transfers
<!-- id: P-LIS-TRANSFER · tags: transfer, airport, pick up, driver, car, taxi, metro -->
A private transfer from Lisbon airport is EUR 60 per car for up to three guests, about 25 minutes. The concierge confirms it once the flight number is received. The airport metro reaches Baixa-Chiado in 30 minutes for guests who prefer it.

## Nearby dining
<!-- id: P-LIS-DINING · tags: restaurant, dining, dinner, recommend, eat, food -->
The concierge recommends Belcanto (two Michelin stars, 3 minutes' walk), Cervejaria Ramiro (seafood, 10 minutes by taxi) and Tágide (Portuguese, river view, 5 minutes' walk). Tables are booked by the concierge; popular places need two to three days' notice.

## Accessibility
<!-- id: P-LIS-ACCESS · tags: accessibility, wheelchair, step-free, accessible room, lift, mobility -->
Two Deluxe Rooms are step-free with roll-in showers and grab rails; the lift serves every floor. The rooftop bar is reached by lift. Access needs are recorded in a restricted trace to front office so the right room is blocked before arrival.

## Getting around
<!-- id: P-LIS-LOCAL · tags: location, tram, neighbourhood, directions, sights, walk -->
The hotel is a 2-minute walk from Baixa-Chiado metro and the number 28 tram. Alfama, the castle and the riverfront are all within 20 minutes on foot. Street noise from the tram line is lowest in River View Rooms, which face the river.
