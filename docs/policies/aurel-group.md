# Aurel Hotels — Group reservations handbook

Hotel: all

These rules apply at every Aurel hotel. A hotel handbook may add detail (prices, hours, local arrangements) but never contradicts this one. Each section carries an id the AI agent cites when it answers from it.

## Cancellation - Flexible rate
<!-- id: P-ALL-CXL-FLEX · tags: cancellation, cancel, refund, flexible, free cancellation, deadline -->
Flexible bookings can be cancelled free of charge until 48 hours before arrival at city hotels and 72 hours before arrival at resorts during peak months (June to September, Christmas and Easter weeks). Later cancellations and no-shows are charged the first night. The cancellation deadline is always shown on the confirmation and counts in the hotel's local time.

## Cancellation - Advance Purchase
<!-- id: P-ALL-CXL-ADV · tags: cancellation, refund, non-refundable, prepaid, advance purchase, exception, compassionate, bereavement, hospital -->
Advance Purchase bookings are prepaid at the time of booking and non-refundable. Exceptions for bereavement, hospitalisation or official travel bans may be granted as a goodwill credit for a future stay, valid twelve months, only by a reservations manager and never by an agent. Agents acknowledge the request with the holding reply and pass the case to the manager the same day. We never ask a guest for medical or other proof in writing.

## Modifications
<!-- id: P-ALL-MOD · tags: modify, change, change dates, move booking, dates, room change, amend -->
Flexible and Stay Longer bookings may be modified at the live published rate for the new dates or room, subject to availability. Advance Purchase bookings cannot be modified by the desk; a reservations manager may rebook as an exception. Rate differences are always charged or refunded at published rates, never waived. A modification is confirmed to the guest only after it is saved in the PMS.

## Rate parity and price matching
<!-- id: P-ALL-PARITY · tags: rate, price, price match, parity, discount, cheaper, ota, booking.com, expedia, best price -->
Published rates on aurelhotels.com match the rates shown on online travel agencies. Agents may not discount, match or undercut a quoted OTA price under any circumstances. Direct bookers receive the Aurel Direct benefits instead: flexible check-out when available, a welcome drink and no booking fee. Non-public, corporate and negotiated rates require a reservations manager.

## Payment details
<!-- id: P-ALL-PAYMENT · tags: payment, card, credit card, cvv, security, pay, deposit, guarantee -->
Card details must never be requested, accepted or stored from email, WhatsApp or OTA messaging. If a guest sends card details, they are removed from the message before it is stored and the guest receives a secure payment link generated by the payment gateway. Deposits and guarantees are taken only through that link or at the hotel.

## Allergy, medical and accessibility information
<!-- id: P-ALL-SENSITIVE · tags: allergy, medical, accessibility, disability, wheelchair, gdpr, sensitive, dietary, health -->
Allergy, medical and accessibility details are special category data under GDPR. Record only what the hotel needs to deliver the stay safely, in a dedicated PMS trace with the restricted flag, never in free-text notes, OTA messages or email forwards. Confirm to the guest that we hold the information and say exactly who at the hotel will see it. Never promise an allergen-free kitchen; promise a briefed chef and a flagged table.

## Groups and events
<!-- id: P-ALL-GROUPS · tags: group, groups, event, wedding, meeting, conference, offsite, block, 8 rooms -->
Requests for eight or more rooms, any event space, weddings, meetings or offsites are handled by the groups and events specialists, who respond within one working day with a single proposal covering rooms, meeting space and dining. The desk sends the fixed acknowledgement only and never quotes a rate or holds inventory for a group.

## Early check-in
<!-- id: P-ALL-EARLY · tags: early check-in, check-in, arrival, early arrival, morning arrival, room ready -->
Early check-in is never guaranteed in advance, in writing or verbally. Front office confirms the evening before arrival based on departures and calls the guest if an early room is possible. Guaranteed early arrival requires booking and paying for the previous night. Luggage storage and use of the facilities are always available from arrival, and guests may change in the spa or pool area where the hotel has one.

## Late check-out
<!-- id: P-ALL-LATE · tags: late check-out, check-out, departure, late departure, extend -->
Late check-out until 14:00 is complimentary when availability allows and is confirmed by front office on the morning of departure, not in advance. Later departures are charged half the nightly rate until 18:00 and a full night after that. Luxury programme guests have late check-out included subject to availability.

## Celebrations and occasions
<!-- id: P-ALL-OCCASION · tags: anniversary, birthday, honeymoon, celebration, occasion, surprise, upgrade, proposal -->
For anniversaries, birthdays, honeymoons and proposals the hotel provides a complimentary in-room celebration touch (a handwritten card with sparkling wine or a small cake) arranged by the hotel team when the desk logs the occasion in a trace. Room upgrades are at the hotel's discretion on the day of arrival and must never be promised in advance. Flowers, cakes with a message and photographers are arranged by the concierge at published prices.

## Cots and extra beds
<!-- id: P-ALL-COT · tags: cot, crib, baby, infant, extra bed, rollaway, children, child, family -->
Cots are complimentary for children under two and are subject to room compatibility shown on the room type. Extra beds are available only in rooms marked for three or more guests and are charged per night at the published supplement. Cots are set up before arrival when requested at least 24 hours ahead; the desk logs a housekeeping trace.

## Travel advisor and luxury programme bookings
<!-- id: P-ALL-VIP · tags: vip, travel advisor, advisor, programme, program, amenity, amenities, upgrade, virtuoso, repeat guest -->
Bookings made through recognised luxury programmes include a standard amenity set: daily breakfast, a hotel credit per stay, early check-in and late check-out subject to availability, and an upgrade on arrival subject to availability. Confirmations to advisors are issued by reservations staff, not by the AI agent, after checking the programme code on the booking. Repeat guests with six or more stays are flagged as VIP and handled by a senior agent.

## Airport transfers
<!-- id: P-ALL-TRANSFER · tags: transfer, airport, pick up, pickup, driver, car, taxi, shuttle, collection -->
Private transfers are arranged by the concierge and charged at the published rate per vehicle, shown in each hotel's handbook. A transfer is only confirmed once the concierge has the flight number and has sent written confirmation with the driver's details. The desk logs a concierge trace and tells the guest the concierge will confirm; it never confirms a car itself.

## Children
<!-- id: P-ALL-CHILDREN · tags: children, child, kids, family, age, babysitting -->
Children of all ages are welcome at every Aurel hotel except Aurel Santorini, which welcomes children aged twelve and over. Children under twelve stay free in their parents' room when using existing bedding. Babysitting can be arranged by the concierge with 24 hours' notice at published rates.

## Smoking
<!-- id: P-ALL-SMOKING · tags: smoking, smoke, cigarette, vape, non-smoking -->
All rooms, suites and villas are non-smoking, including balconies and terraces. Designated outdoor smoking areas exist at every hotel. A deep-cleaning charge applies when a room has been smoked in.

## Invoices and receipts
<!-- id: P-ALL-INVOICE · tags: invoice, receipt, bill, vat, company, tax -->
Invoices are issued at check-out and can be addressed to a company with the VAT number given before departure. Copies of past invoices are sent by the hotel's front office within two working days; the desk logs a front-office trace rather than promising a date.

## Lost property
<!-- id: P-ALL-LOST · tags: lost, forgot, left behind, lost property, item -->
Items left behind are kept by housekeeping for three months. Returns are sent by courier at the guest's cost once the guest confirms the address and the item. The desk logs a housekeeping trace with a description; it never confirms an item has been found.
