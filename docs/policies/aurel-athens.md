# Aurel Athens — Hotel handbook

Hotel: ATH

City hotel in Plaka, Athens. Rooms: Deluxe Room, Acropolis View Room, Acropolis Suite. Group rules in the group handbook apply.

## Check-in and check-out times
<!-- id: P-ATH-HOURS · tags: check-in, check-out, time, times, hours, arrival, departure, luggage -->
Check-in at Aurel Athens is from 15:00 and check-out is by 12:00. Luggage can be stored before check-in and after check-out, and guests may use the fitness room and rooftop from arrival. Reception is open 24 hours.

## Facilities
<!-- id: P-ATH-FACILITIES · tags: gym, fitness, pool, spa, wifi, wi-fi, breakfast, facilities, restaurant, bar, rooftop -->
Aurel Athens has a fitness room open 24 hours, complimentary Wi-Fi throughout, a restaurant and a rooftop bar facing the Acropolis, open 17:00 to 01:00. Breakfast is served 07:00 to 10:30 on the rooftop in summer. There is no swimming pool or spa.

## Parking
<!-- id: P-ATH-PARK · tags: parking, car, garage, drive -->
Underground parking beneath the hotel at EUR 28 per night, no reservation needed, height limit 2.0 m. Plaka is largely pedestrian; the concierge sends arrival directions for the garage entrance on request.

## Pets
<!-- id: P-ATH-PETS · tags: pets, pet, dog, dogs, cat, animal -->
Dogs up to 10 kg are welcome in Deluxe Rooms at EUR 35 per night, maximum one per room. Dogs are not admitted to the rooftop restaurant. Other animals are not accepted except certified assistance animals, which stay free.

## Airport transfers
<!-- id: P-ATH-TRANSFER · tags: transfer, airport, pick up, driver, car, taxi, metro -->
A private transfer from Athens airport is EUR 70 per car for up to three guests, 40 to 50 minutes. The concierge confirms it once the flight number is received. Official taxis charge a fixed EUR 40 by day and EUR 55 at night.

## Nearby dining
<!-- id: P-ATH-DINING · tags: restaurant, dining, dinner, recommend, eat, taverna -->
The concierge recommends Strofi (rooftop taverna with Acropolis view, 8 minutes' walk), Nolan (modern Greek, 10 minutes' walk) and Diporto for a traditional lunch. Tables are booked by the concierge.

## Accessibility
<!-- id: P-ATH-ACCESS · tags: accessibility, wheelchair, step-free, accessible room, lift, mobility -->
Two Deluxe Rooms are step-free with roll-in showers; the rooftop is reached by lift. Plaka's streets are steep and cobbled, which matters for wheelchair users; the concierge can arrange step-free routes to the Acropolis Museum. Access needs are recorded in a restricted trace to front office.

## Getting around
<!-- id: P-ATH-LOCAL · tags: location, neighbourhood, directions, acropolis, walk, noise -->
The Acropolis entrance is a 10-minute walk, Syntagma metro 7 minutes. Acropolis View Rooms face the hill and are quiet; Deluxe Rooms face a pedestrian lane with evening restaurant noise until about midnight.
