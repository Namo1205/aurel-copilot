# Aurel Madrid — Hotel handbook

Hotel: MAD

City hotel beside the Retiro park, Madrid. Rooms: Deluxe Room, Premier Room, Retiro Suite. Group rules in the group handbook apply.

## Check-in and check-out times
<!-- id: P-MAD-HOURS · tags: check-in, check-out, time, times, hours, arrival, departure, luggage -->
Check-in at Aurel Madrid is from 15:00 and check-out is by 12:00. Luggage can be stored before check-in and after check-out, and guests may use the fitness room and bar from arrival. Reception is open 24 hours.

## Facilities
<!-- id: P-MAD-FACILITIES · tags: gym, fitness, pool, spa, wifi, wi-fi, breakfast, facilities, restaurant, bar, terrace -->
Aurel Madrid has a fitness room open 24 hours, complimentary Wi-Fi throughout, a restaurant and a courtyard bar open 12:00 to midnight. Breakfast is served 07:00 to 10:30, until 11:00 at weekends. There is no swimming pool or spa.

## Parking
<!-- id: P-MAD-PARK · tags: parking, car, garage, valet, drive -->
Underground garage beneath the hotel at EUR 38 per night, height limit 1.9 m, no reservation needed. Valet is available from the main entrance between 07:00 and 23:00. Madrid's low-emission zone covers the hotel; the concierge can advise on permits for non-resident cars.

## Pets
<!-- id: P-MAD-PETS · tags: pets, pet, dog, dogs, cat, animal -->
Dogs up to 15 kg are welcome in Deluxe and Premier Rooms at EUR 40 per night, maximum one per room. Retiro park across the road allows dogs on a lead. Other animals are not accepted except certified assistance animals, which stay free.

## Airport transfers
<!-- id: P-MAD-TRANSFER · tags: transfer, airport, pick up, driver, car, taxi, barajas -->
A private transfer from Madrid-Barajas is EUR 75 per car for up to three guests, 30 to 45 minutes depending on traffic. The concierge confirms it once the flight number is received. Official taxis from the airport charge a fixed EUR 33 to the city centre.

## Nearby dining
<!-- id: P-MAD-DINING · tags: restaurant, dining, dinner, recommend, eat, tapas -->
The concierge recommends La Tasquería (modern Spanish, 10 minutes' walk), Horcher (classic, opposite the Retiro) and Casa Dani in the Mercado de la Paz for lunch. Madrid dines late; most kitchens open at 20:30.

## Accessibility
<!-- id: P-MAD-ACCESS · tags: accessibility, wheelchair, step-free, accessible room, lift, mobility -->
Three Deluxe Rooms are step-free with roll-in showers; all public areas are reached by lift or ramp. Access needs are recorded in a restricted trace to front office so the right room is blocked before arrival.

## Getting around
<!-- id: P-MAD-LOCAL · tags: location, metro, neighbourhood, directions, museum, prado, walk -->
The Prado is a 7-minute walk and the Retiro park is across the road. Retiro metro station is 3 minutes away. The hotel is quiet at night; rooms facing the courtyard are the quietest.
