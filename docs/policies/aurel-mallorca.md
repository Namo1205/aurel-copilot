# Aurel Mallorca — Hotel handbook

Hotel: MLL

Cliffside resort in Deià, Mallorca. Rooms: Garden Room, Sea View Room, Family Suite, Cliff Villa. Group rules in the group handbook apply.

## Check-in and check-out times
<!-- id: P-MLL-HOURS · tags: check-in, check-out, time, times, hours, arrival, departure, luggage -->
Check-in at Aurel Mallorca is from 15:00 and check-out is by 12:00. Luggage can be stored before check-in and after check-out, and guests may use the pool, spa and restaurant from arrival; changing rooms are in the spa. Reception is open 24 hours.

## Facilities
<!-- id: P-MLL-FACILITIES · tags: gym, fitness, pool, spa, wifi, wi-fi, breakfast, facilities, restaurant, bar, terrace -->
Aurel Mallorca has an outdoor pool, a fitness room open 06:00 to 22:00, a spa with treatment rooms and a thermal area, a restaurant and a terrace bar, and complimentary Wi-Fi throughout. Breakfast is served 07:30 to 11:00 on the terrace in summer. The pool is open 08:00 to 20:00 and is unheated.

## Parking
<!-- id: P-MLL-PARK · tags: parking, car, garage, drive, rental car -->
Free parking on site for all guests. A car is recommended for exploring the Tramuntana; the hotel can arrange a rental delivered to the door. The road from Palma takes about 40 minutes and is winding.

## Pets
<!-- id: P-MLL-PETS · tags: pets, pet, dog, dogs, cat, animal -->
Dogs up to 15 kg are welcome in Garden Rooms and Cliff Villas at EUR 45 per night, maximum one per room. Dogs are not accepted in Sea View Rooms or Family Suites and are not admitted to the pool terrace or restaurant. Other animals are not accepted except certified assistance animals, which stay free.

## Airport transfers
<!-- id: P-MLL-TRANSFER · tags: transfer, airport, pick up, driver, car, taxi, palma -->
A private transfer from Palma airport is EUR 120 per car for up to three guests, about 45 minutes. The concierge confirms it once the flight number is received.

## Dietary requirements
<!-- id: P-MLL-DIET · tags: allergy, allergies, dietary, nut, gluten, dairy, vegan, restaurant, kitchen -->
The kitchen can accommodate nut, gluten and dairy allergies and vegan diets with 24 hours' notice. Severe allergies are flagged to the executive chef and noted on the table card for every reservation during the stay, including breakfast. We never describe the kitchen as allergen-free; we promise a briefed chef and a flagged table.

## Spa bookings
<!-- id: P-MLL-SPA · tags: spa, treatment, massage, wellness, thermal -->
Spa treatments can be pre-booked by the concierge up to the day before arrival. Guests under 16 are not admitted to the thermal area. Treatments cancelled with less than 24 hours' notice are charged in full.

## Nearby dining
<!-- id: P-MLL-DINING · tags: restaurant, dining, dinner, recommend, eat, michelin -->
The concierge recommends Es Racó d'es Teix (Michelin, 5 minutes' walk), Ca's Patró March (seafood at the cala, 15 minutes' walk down a steep path) and Sebastian (modern Mediterranean, in the village). Reservations are made by the concierge; in summer allow several days.

## Children and families
<!-- id: P-MLL-CHILD · tags: children, child, kids, family, cot, babysitting -->
Children of all ages are welcome. Family Suites sleep two adults and two children; cots fit in Family Suites and Cliff Villas. The cliff paths and pool are unsupervised and not fenced, so young children need an adult with them outdoors. Babysitting with 24 hours' notice.

## Accessibility
<!-- id: P-MLL-ACCESS · tags: accessibility, wheelchair, step-free, accessible room, mobility, steps -->
The site is terraced into a cliff and most routes involve steps. One Garden Room near reception is step-free with a roll-in shower and level access to the restaurant and pool. Sea View Rooms and the cala path are not suitable for wheelchairs. Access needs are recorded in a restricted trace to front office.
