# Aurel Amalfi — Hotel handbook

Hotel: AMF

Clifftop resort in Positano, Amalfi Coast. Rooms: Classic Room, Sea View Room, Panorama Suite. Group rules in the group handbook apply.

## Check-in and check-out times
<!-- id: P-AMF-HOURS · tags: check-in, check-out, time, times, hours, arrival, departure, luggage -->
Check-in at Aurel Amalfi is from 15:00 and check-out is by 11:00. Luggage can be stored before check-in and after check-out, and guests may use the pool, spa and restaurant from arrival. Porters carry luggage from the road; the hotel is reached by stairs or the funicular lift. Reception is open 24 hours.

## Facilities
<!-- id: P-AMF-FACILITIES · tags: gym, fitness, pool, spa, wifi, wi-fi, breakfast, facilities, restaurant, bar, terrace -->
Aurel Amalfi has an outdoor pool, a fitness room open 06:00 to 22:00, a spa with treatment rooms and a thermal area, a terrace restaurant and bar, and complimentary Wi-Fi throughout. Breakfast is served 07:30 to 11:00 on the terrace. The hotel closes from November to March.

## Parking
<!-- id: P-AMF-PARK · tags: parking, car, garage, drive -->
No parking at the hotel. A partner garage in Positano charges EUR 50 per day and the concierge reserves a space with the registration number. We recommend arriving without a car; the coast road is slow and narrow in summer.

## Pets
<!-- id: P-AMF-PETS · tags: pets, pet, dog, dogs, cat, animal -->
Dogs up to 10 kg are welcome in Classic Rooms at EUR 45 per night, maximum one per room. Dogs are not admitted to the pool terrace or restaurant. Other animals are not accepted except certified assistance animals, which stay free.

## Airport transfers
<!-- id: P-AMF-TRANSFER · tags: transfer, airport, pick up, driver, car, taxi, naples, ferry -->
A private transfer from Naples airport is EUR 220 per car for up to three guests, about 90 minutes; a minivan for up to six is EUR 320. Confirmed by the concierge once the flight number is received. From April to October the ferry from Naples to Positano takes 90 minutes and the concierge can book seats.

## Spa bookings
<!-- id: P-AMF-SPA · tags: spa, treatment, massage, wellness, thermal -->
Spa treatments can be pre-booked by the concierge up to the day before arrival. Guests under 16 are not admitted to the thermal area. Treatments cancelled with less than 24 hours' notice are charged in full.

## Nearby dining
<!-- id: P-AMF-DINING · tags: restaurant, dining, dinner, recommend, eat, seafood -->
The concierge recommends La Sponda (one Michelin star, candlelit, 5 minutes' walk), Da Adolfo (beach seafood reached by boat shuttle) and Chez Black on the beach. Tables are booked by the concierge; in summer allow a week for La Sponda.

## Sea view and room allocation
<!-- id: P-AMF-VIEW · tags: sea view, view, room allocation, balcony, terrace -->
All Sea View Rooms and Panorama Suites face the sea with a private balcony or terrace. Classic Rooms face the village or garden. The view category is guaranteed; the specific room is allocated on the day of arrival.

## Children and families
<!-- id: P-AMF-CHILD · tags: children, child, kids, family, cot, babysitting -->
Children of all ages are welcome. Cots fit in Sea View Rooms and Panorama Suites but not in Classic Rooms. The pool and terraces are unsupervised and have sheer drops behind railings, so young children need an adult outdoors.

## Accessibility
<!-- id: P-AMF-ACCESS · tags: accessibility, wheelchair, step-free, accessible room, mobility, steps, lift -->
The hotel is built into the cliff and reached by a funicular lift from the road; inside, every level is served by lifts. One Sea View Room is step-free with a roll-in shower. Positano itself is almost entirely steps. Access needs are recorded in a restricted trace to front office.
