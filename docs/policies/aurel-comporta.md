# Aurel Comporta — Hotel handbook

Hotel: COM

Beach resort in Comporta, Portugal. Rooms: Garden Room, Dune Villa, Ocean Villa. Group rules in the group handbook apply.

## Check-in and check-out times
<!-- id: P-COM-HOURS · tags: check-in, check-out, time, times, hours, arrival, departure, luggage -->
Check-in at Aurel Comporta is from 16:00 and check-out is by 12:00. Luggage can be stored before check-in and after check-out, and guests may use the pool, spa and beach club from arrival; changing rooms and showers are in the spa. Reception is open 07:00 to 23:00, with a night porter after that.

## Facilities
<!-- id: P-COM-FACILITIES · tags: gym, fitness, pool, spa, wifi, wi-fi, breakfast, facilities, restaurant, bar, beach -->
Aurel Comporta has an outdoor pool heated from April to October, a fitness room open 06:00 to 22:00, a spa with treatment rooms and a thermal area, a beach club with a shuttle every 20 minutes, and complimentary Wi-Fi throughout. Breakfast is served 07:30 to 11:00. The restaurant serves lunch at the beach club and dinner at the main house.

## Parking
<!-- id: P-COM-PARK · tags: parking, car, garage, drive, rental car -->
Free parking on site for all guests, with electric charging at no extra cost. A car is recommended; Comporta has no public transport and the nearest town is 15 minutes' drive.

## Pets
<!-- id: P-COM-PETS · tags: pets, pet, dog, dogs, cat, animal -->
Dogs of any size are welcome in Dune Villas and Ocean Villas at EUR 50 per night, maximum two per villa, and may use the villa garden and the dog-friendly stretch of beach. Dogs are not accepted in Garden Rooms or in the pool area. Other animals are not accepted except certified assistance animals, which stay free.

## Airport transfers
<!-- id: P-COM-TRANSFER · tags: transfer, airport, pick up, driver, car, taxi, lisbon -->
A private transfer from Lisbon airport is EUR 180 per car for up to three guests, about 75 minutes. A minivan for up to six is EUR 260. The concierge confirms it once the flight number is received.

## Spa bookings
<!-- id: P-COM-SPA · tags: spa, treatment, massage, wellness, thermal -->
Spa treatments can be pre-booked by the concierge up to the day before arrival; popular afternoon slots in summer fill a week ahead. The thermal area is for guests aged 16 and over. Treatments cancelled with less than 24 hours' notice are charged in full.

## Nearby dining
<!-- id: P-COM-DINING · tags: restaurant, dining, dinner, recommend, eat, seafood -->
The concierge recommends Sal (beachfront seafood, 10 minutes' drive), Cavalariça (modern Portuguese in the village) and Dona Bia for a traditional lunch. Tables are booked by the concierge; summer weekends need three to four days' notice.

## Children and families
<!-- id: P-COM-CHILD · tags: children, child, kids, family, cot, babysitting, kids club -->
Children of all ages are welcome. Cots fit in Dune Villas and Ocean Villas but not in Garden Rooms. A kids' club runs 10:00 to 17:00 in July and August for ages 4 to 12. Babysitting can be arranged with 24 hours' notice. The pool is unsupervised.

## Accessibility
<!-- id: P-COM-ACCESS · tags: accessibility, wheelchair, step-free, accessible room, mobility, sand -->
Two Garden Rooms and one Dune Villa are step-free with roll-in showers and are on the boardwalk to the main house. The beach is reached by a boardwalk and a beach wheelchair is available on request. Access needs are recorded in a restricted trace to front office.
